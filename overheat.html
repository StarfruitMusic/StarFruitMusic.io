OVERHEAT

>> DEVELOPER: FISHY STUDIOZ >> ROLE: AUDIO & OST >> ENGINE: UNITY

A movement shooter taking place in a post-singularity world, designed as a Beat 'em up game.

Drawing inspiration from Tsutomu Nihei's abstract megastructures and games like God Hand and Titanfall 2, Overheat fulfills the hyper-mobile super soldier fantasy. The game recently migrated from Roblox to Unity to fully realize its complex melee-driven combat loops.

(Gameplay Pillars)

01. Brawling

Gundiver utilizes a wide range of martial arts moves for close-quarters combat. Featuring a unique "bounce back" mechanic, melee attacks never halt or reset player momentum, allowing combos to become a seamless extension of movement.

02. Gunplay

Blending the punchy feel of boomer shooters with modern polish. Weapon slots are limited to two, but each operates as a "hybrid" with multiple unique functions—expanding skill expression without overwhelming the player.

03. Mobility

Sliding is fully steerable, almost like drifting a car. Dashing is omni-directional. Topped with a fine-tuned momentum conservation system, the game demands fast reaction times and rapid decision-making.

Our Audio Work

A game with action this fast-paced requires a soundtrack that matches its frantic heartbeat. We provide a complete original soundtrack and dynamic sound design tailored to the chaotic combo-driven gameplay.
